01What do I need to provide?

Start with the lead list you want to work and your ICP direction. Fulgurite evaluates, researches, scores, prioritizes, writes, and sends on your behalf.

02How does Fulgurite prioritize my leads?

Fulgurite combines ICP fit, contact relevance, signal quality, signal recency, and timing to score the leads already on your list.

03How is the sending order set?

The score sets the automatic sending order. The strongest opportunities are contacted first instead of being spread randomly across the campaign.

04Does Fulgurite send the emails?

Yes. Fulgurite prepares the sequence and sends it through your connected email account according to the campaign order.

05Who handles replies and meetings?

Your team owns replies, sales conversations, meetings, opportunities, and closing. Fulgurite is responsible for the outbound workflow that starts those conversations.

06Does Fulgurite guarantee replies or meetings?

No. Fulgurite improves the quality, timing, and order of outreach, but outcomes also depend on your market, offer, list, sales process, and follow-through.
