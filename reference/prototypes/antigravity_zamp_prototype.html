Gate Policy

Control the strictness of the Confidence Gate across this workspace. This defines how much evidence the AI needs before drafting.

Strict (Default)

Active

Requires direct evidence connecting the prospect's pain to your value prop (e.g., Authored Content or highly relevant Business Event). Expect higher refusal rates.

Balanced

Will draft based on broader firmographic signals (e.g., "Companies of this size in this vertical usually face this pain").

Will draft an email even on weak signals, but will route all of them to the Research Backlog instead of auto-approving.
